How to use these sessions

  1. Open a session and sign in with class code Y13BIO and your first and last name. Use the same spelling every time: that is how your work is found again on any computer.
  2. Each section starts with a note-making brief. Make your own notes from the textbook, then click I have made my notes to unlock the quick-check questions. You get one attempt at each.
  3. Write full answers to the exam questions before you reveal the mark scheme, then mark yourself honestly.
  4. Your work saves automatically and your teacher can see it. Click Back up my work before you close the page.
